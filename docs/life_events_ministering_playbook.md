# Major Life-Events & Ministering Care Playbook (Companion Draft)
**Saratoga Springs Utah Crossroads Stake — Phase 2 Companion Resource**  
*For Bishoprics, Ward Relief Society Presidencies, Elders Quorum Presidencies, Ministering Companions, and Stake Support Specialists*

> **Why This Is Separate from the Stake Emergency Operations Plan:**  
> The Stake Emergency Operations Plan (`GH 22.9.1.3`) activates for **community-wide disasters** (earthquakes, wildfires, blizzards, hazmat plumes) where civic infrastructure fails across many homes at once. This companion guide addresses **everyday personal and family life transitions** (`GH 21`, `22.4–22.8`) that happen every week in every ward—using the same clean **4-Horizon × 3-Tier** structure (**Preparation → Short-Term → Mid-Term → Long-Term** across **Individual/Family → Ward & Ministering → Stake Support**).

---

## Core Stewardship & Care Guardrails (*General Handbook*)

1. **Strict Confidentiality & Zero Gossip (`GH 22.4.5`):** Ask before sharing *any* medical, financial, marital, or loss details outside the Bishopric/RS/EQ Presidency. Fast offerings, Bishops' Orders, and self-reliance plans are sacred and private.
2. **Immediate Safety & Abuse Zero-Tolerance (`GH 38.6.2`):** If abuse or physical danger is ever disclosed or suspected, prioritize immediate safety (`911`), and Bishops call the **24/7 Church Abuse Help Line (`1-800-453-3860`)** promptly.
3. **Shared EQ + RS Load (`GH 22.6.2`):** Relief Society and Elders Quorum Presidencies serve as co-equal frontline partners—sharing meals, errands, childcare, heavy lifting, and check-ins rather than overloading one presidency.
4. **Guard Against "Compassion Drop-Off":** Week-one casseroles are easy; **Months 3–12** (cancer treatment, house-fire rebuilds, widowhood, divorce recovery, job-search fatigue) are when families feel most alone. Put quiet recurring check-ins on the calendar.

### 1. Sudden Job Loss / Major Income Disruption
*Governing Handbook Standard:* `GH 22.1.4` (Temporal Preparedness), `GH 22.4–22.6` (Welfare & Self-Reliance Plan), `GH 22.9.4` & `22.13` (Stake Specialists & Church Employment/Self-Reliance Resources).

| Horizon | Individual & Family (First) | Ward & Ministering (Second) | Stake Support (Third) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Preparation (Before)** | Pay tithes/offerings, live on a budget, minimize debt, build a **1→3 month emergency fund** (`GH 22.1.4`), keep a 2-week to 3-month rotating everyday pantry, maintain an updated resume & network, and review disability/health coverage. | Teach `GH 22.1.4` temporal self-reliance periodically (5th Sunday/activities); cultivate warm ministering trust so households share financial anxiety early; invite members to *Personal Finances* & *Find a Better Job* groups. | Call & train a **Stake Welfare/Self-Reliance & Employment Specialist** (`GH 22.9.4`); run recurring Stake Self-Reliance courses (`GH 22.13`); maintain direct ties to Church Employment Services & local employers. |
| **Short-Term (0–72 Hours / Week 1)** | Pause all non-essential spending immediately; verify final paycheck, severance & COBRA/Marketplace/Medicaid health-insurance deadlines; file **Utah Unemployment (`jobs.utah.gov`)**; counsel in prayer and contact Bishop/EQ/RS confidentially if urgent rent/food gaps exist. | Listen with warmth and zero judgment; **guard strict confidentiality (`GH 22.4.5`)**; Bishop (`GH 22.6.1`) assesses immediate obligations and delegates RS/EQ President (`GH 22.6.2`) to help prepare a **Bishops' Order** (*Saratoga Springs Bishops' Storehouse, 142 W Medical Dr*) if pantry needs are immediate. | With member's permission, connect Bishop/member directly to **Stake Employment Specialist** or `employment.ChurchofJesusChrist.org`; share upcoming Stake Self-Reliance group dates. |
| **Mid-Term (1–4 Weeks)** | Shift to a bare-bones crisis budget using home pantry + emergency fund; complete a **Self-Reliance Plan** with the Bishop (if receiving Church aid); run a structured daily job search (resumes, networking, bridge/temp work); check `211 Utah` / utility assistance programs. | Ministering companions offer steady friendship and quiet practical help (childcare for interviews, resume feedback—*only with consent*); Ward Council shares job leads *without* disclosing confidential fast-offering details; Bishop periodically reviews self-reliance progress & assistance sustainability. | Stake Employment Specialist provides 1-on-1 resume/interview coaching and cross-ward professional introductions; coordinate **Deseret Industries** job-readiness or **BYU-Pathway / vocational training** referrals if a career pivot is needed; align Bishops in **Stake Bishops' Council (`GH 22.11`)**. |
| **Long-Term (2+ Months)** | Transition off temporary assistance once income stabilizes; rebuild the 1–3 month emergency fund and depleted pantry gradually without debt; join *Personal Finances* or *Education for Better Work* to harden long-term career resilience. | Celebrate re-employment quietly; continue ministering check-ins during the 6–12 month financial catch-up phase; support family emotional recovery and youth stress. | Sustain year-round Stake Self-Reliance groups (`GH 22.13`) and optional cross-ward career mentoring/networking evenings. |

### 2. Residential House Fire (Single-Family Home, Townhome, or Apartment)
*Governing Local Lifelines:* Saratoga Springs Fire (`911` / `801-766-6505`), **American Red Cross Utah Fire Relief (`801-373-8580`)**, **Deseret Industries (`104 W Medical Dr`)**, **Saratoga Springs Bishops' Storehouse (`142 W Medical Dr • 801-436-8205`)**.

| Horizon | Individual & Family (First) | Ward & Ministering (Second) | Stake Support (Third) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Preparation (Before)** | Test smoke & CO alarms monthly; keep ABC fire extinguishers on every level; practice two exits per bedroom and set a **"Right Outside Your Home"** meeting spot; keep **72-hr go-bags by exit**, cloud/waterproof backups of IDs/deeds, room-by-room home inventory video, and active **homeowner's or renter's insurance**. | Teach fire-safety & renter's insurance reminders during Q2/Q3 preparedness touchpoints; keep Member Tools updated; know SN-3 mobility households needing rapid exit help. | Maintain verified contacts for Saratoga Springs Fire, **American Red Cross** (immediate single-family fire relief vouchers), Deseret Industries, and Bishops' Storehouse. |
| **Short-Term (0–72 Hours)** | **Get out, stay out, call 911** (never re-enter for belongings or pets); gather at outside meeting spot; accept immediate **American Red Cross** lodging/essentials voucher; open an insurance claim right away; notify Bishop / RS or EQ President once safe. | Immediate comforting presence & priesthood blessings; secure **night-one shelter** (Red Cross/insurance hotel, fast-offering bridge, or safe **ward host family** using 5-point safety checklist); mobilize immediate clothing, shoes, toiletries, kids' comfort items & pet boarding via ministering + Bishop-approved **Deseret Industries / Storehouse** orders. | If a multi-unit townhome/apartment fire displaces multiple families, Stake Presidency & Stake RS Presidency assist with cross-ward host homes, expedited Storehouse/DI support, and Area/FM coordination if any Church facility assistance is requested. |
| **Mid-Term (1–4 Weeks)** | Secure mid-term housing (insurance Additional Living Expense / ALE rental); document losses with adjuster using cloud inventory; replace IDs, prescriptions, glasses, and school gear; pace emotional recovery for children. | Organize sustainable meal calendar, school carpools, laundry, and a **safe volunteer salvage/pack-out crew** (*strictly AFTER Fire Marshal & adjuster clear the site*—N95s/gloves required, zero youth in soot/structural areas); Bishop evaluates fast-offering help for deductibles or uninsured renter losses (`GH 22.6.1`). | With family consent, connect them to vetted Stake volunteer mentors (insurance adjusters, licensed contractors, realtors) to prevent predatory contractor scams; facilitate **LDS Family Services** trauma counseling referrals through the Bishop. |
| **Long-Term (2+ Months)** | Oversee licensed rebuild or permanent move; replenish furnishings gradually (leveraging insurance/DI as appropriate); review coverage limits and rebuild 72-hr kits & cloud backups. | **Guard against compassion drop-off**—house-fire displacement typically lasts **6–12 months**; sustain quiet monthly ministering check-ins and organize a joyful move-back-in service day. | Share anonymized fire-prevention lessons (dryer vents, lithium-battery charging safety, renter's insurance) during annual Stake preparedness touchpoints. |

### 3. Protracted Medical Emergency / Critical Illness
*Covers Cancer, Major Trauma, Surgery, Extended Hospitalization, or Severe Mental Health Crisis.*  
*Governing Local Lifelines:* Intermountain Saratoga Springs ER (`801-763-3500`), Primary Children's Lehi (`385-531-1200`), Holy Cross Mountain Point (`385-345-3000`), **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**, **LDS Family Services**, **211 Utah**.

| Horizon | Individual & Family (First) | Ward & Ministering (Second) | Stake Support (Third) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Preparation (Before)** | Review health, disability & life insurance annually; keep a **2–3 week Rx buffer** and a **1-page Medication, Allergy & Doctor Card** (wallet, fridge, phone); complete a **Utah Advance Health Care Directive**; name trusted childcare/pet backups; maintain SN-1 backup power if needed; build a 1–3 month emergency fund. | Cultivate genuine ministering friendships *before* crisis hits; quietly note chronic/caregiver vulnerabilities on confidential Form W-1 via quarterly ministering interviews (`GH 21.3`); maintain a ready network of vetted childcare, ride, and meal volunteers. | Maintain active referral pathways with **LDS Family Services**, local hospitals, `211 Utah` / `988`, and recurring Stake **Emotional Resilience** Self-Reliance groups (`GH 22.13`). |
| **Short-Term (0–72 Hours)** | Focus 100% on acute medical care (`911` / ER / specialists) and priesthood blessings; appoint **one family spokesperson or trusted friend** to relay updates (shields exhausted family from dozens of texts); activate pre-arranged childcare/pet coverage. | Bishopric provides blessings and pastoral care; RS & EQ Presidencies immediately mobilize **quiet household coverage** (urgent childcare, hospital rides, porch-drop meals for kids at home, snow/yard care) while **strictly honoring medical privacy (`GH 22.4.5` — share zero details without explicit family consent)**. | Quietly broker urgent cross-ward voluntary loans (e.g., SN-1 portable power station) via Ward contacts; connect Bishop with **LDS Family Services** consultation line for acute psychiatric, addiction, or trauma crises. |
| **Mid-Term (1–4+ Weeks)** | Engage a **hospital social worker / patient financial advocate** early (FMLA, short-term disability, charity-care/payment plans); accept specific Ward help without guilt; protect primary caregiver sleep and mental health. | Shift from sprint to **sustainable marathon rhythm**: 2 scheduled meals/week honoring dietary/chemo restrictions, recurring treatment/PT rides, EQ + adult-supervised youth yard/house chores, and **scheduled respite breaks for the primary caregiver**; Bishop assesses wage-loss or medical-bill gaps for fast-offering / Storehouse aid (`GH 22.6.1`). | Broker cross-ward voluntary equipment loans (wheelchair ramps, hospital beds, medical-billing volunteer mentors) via Ward leaders; invite weary family members (when ready) to **Emotional Resilience** groups (`GH 22.13`). |
| **Long-Term (2+ Months)** | Negotiate medical balances and adapt household budget/work to any lasting limitations; establish a sustainable "new normal"; use ongoing counseling or bereavement support (`LDS Family Services`, licensed therapists, `988`). | **Stay present through months 3–12** (when outside meals stop but caregiver fatigue peaks); update confidential Form W-1 if new mobility/power/caregiver needs remain; if bereavement occurs, surround the family patiently through holidays and anniversaries. | Sustain Stake **Emotional Resilience** & **Personal Finances** courses (`GH 22.13`), ongoing LDS Family Services pathways via Bishops, and Care Center / Riverview Branch support if long-term rehab or senior care becomes necessary. |

### 4. Birth or Adoption of a New Child
*Governing Handbook Standard:* `GH 18.6` (Naming & Blessing a Child), `GH 22.6.2` (Compassionate Service & Ministering), `SN-7` (Infants, Expectant & Postpartum Mothers).

| Horizon | Individual & Family (First) | Ward & Ministering (Second) | Stake Support (Third) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Preparation (Before)** | Verify health insurance maternity/adoption coverage & add-dependent deadline (typically **30 days** post-birth/placement); prep freezer meals; pack hospital bag + baby 72-hr essentials (diapers, wipes, blankets, ready-to-feed formula backup); arrange older-sibling & pet care for delivery day; update budget for new costs (`GH 22.1.4`). | Quietly note due date / placement window via ministering sisters & Ward RS; coordinate pre-arranged older-sibling care and postpartum meal sign-up; remind parents gently to keep Member Tools updated after baby arrives. | Maintain Stake/Ward awareness of local **Saratoga Springs Bishops' Storehouse** infant supplies (formula/diapers for welfare needs) and **Primary Children's Hospital (Miller Campus, Lehi • 385-531-1200)** pediatric ER lifeline. |
| **Short-Term (0–72 Hours)** | Focus on mother & baby medical recovery, rest, and bonding; lean on pre-arranged sibling/pet helpers; watch for urgent postpartum red flags (heavy bleeding, high fever, severe headache/blood pressure — call OB or `911`). | Deliver pre-arranged **porch-drop or quiet short-visit meals** (respecting rest and zero expectation to host visitors!); help with older-sibling school drop-offs, errands, or lawn/snow care; Bishop schedules child's name & blessing (`GH 18.6`) when parents are ready. | Rarely needed unless NICU/medical complication occurs (if so, immediately bridge to **Playbook 3** for hospital/medical-crisis support). |
| **Mid-Term (1–4 Weeks)** | Enroll newborn/adopted child on health insurance before the 30-day window closes; obtain birth certificate/SSN and add encrypted copies to emergency document folder; watch honestly for **postpartum depression/anxiety** and speak up early to doctor/OB. | Sustain 1–2 weeks of practical help (meals, grocery runs, holding baby so mom sleeps/showers); ministering sisters check warmly on **emotional/postpartum wellness** (not just the baby!); Ward Clerk creates child's membership record & updates Form W-1 `SN-7` if infant formula/special medical needs exist. | If severe postpartum depression/anxiety or adoption-transition strain arises, connect Bishop with **LDS Family Services** or local perinatal mental health resources. |
| **Long-Term (2+ Months)** | Add infant/toddler gear, formula/baby food, extra clean water (**+1 gal/day** if formula-fed), and seasonal clothes to your **72-hr kit** (rotate every 6 months at Daylight Saving Time); update life insurance, beneficiaries, and guardian designation in your will. | Welcome the growing family warmly into Nursery/Primary rhythms; keep ministering check-ins steady during sleep-deprived months 2–6; include family in Ward preparedness & Self-Reliance touchpoints. | Offer Stake *Personal Finances* & *Emotional Resilience* Self-Reliance courses (`GH 22.13`) as young families adjust budgets and energy. |

### 5. Miscarriage, Stillbirth, or Infant Loss
*Governing Handbook Standard:* `GH 29.5` (Funeral/Memorial Services), `GH 38.7.3` (Stillborn Children — comforting doctrinal guidance & optional memorial/temple reassurance), `GH 22.4.5` (Strict Confidentiality & Dignity).

| Horizon | Individual & Family (First) | Ward & Ministering (Second) | Stake Support (Third) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Preparation (Before)** | Know OB/midwife urgent-care instructions and nearest 24/7 ER (**Intermountain Saratoga Springs ER • 801-763-3500**); build trusting relationships with a couple of close friends or ministering sisters you can text if you ever need quiet help. | Cultivate a ward culture of deep empathy where sisters never feel pressured by insensitive questions about family size; train RS/EQ presidencies on **GH 38.7.3** so leaders offer comforting, accurate gospel hope without speculation. | Keep **LDS Family Services** grief/perinatal-loss counseling contacts and **988** readily available to Bishops. |
| **Short-Term (0–72 Hours)** | Prioritize the mother's physical safety & medical follow-up; accept quiet practical help without needing to explain details; ask for priesthood blessings if desired; tell one trusted person what you do/don't want shared with the ward. | **Strictly guard privacy (`GH 22.4.5`)**—share nothing with the ward unless the couple explicitly asks; drop off **quiet porch-drop meals/comfort items** with *zero* pressure to talk; **avoid clichés or platitudes** (simply say *"I am so sorry; we love you"*); offer childcare for older siblings during medical appointments; for stillbirth/infant loss, Bishop sensitively offers optional memorial/graveside service (`GH 38.7.3`, `29.5`). | Only involved if the Bishop requests Stake Presidency pastoral consultation or **LDS Family Services** crisis/grief support. |
| **Mid-Term (1–4 Weeks)** | Allow physical hormone/body recovery and space for *both* parents to grieve differently; seek medical follow-up and professional grief counseling if sorrow feels overwhelming. | Quietly check in by text/note without expecting immediate replies; be especially mindful on **Mother's Day, Father's Day, and Ward baby blessings**; continue quiet meals/errands if physical recovery is prolonged. | Provide **LDS Family Services** grief referral pathway via the Bishop as requested. |
| **Long-Term (2+ Months)** | Honor grief honestly at the **original due date and 1-year anniversary**; lean on temple worship, prayer, and counseling as needed. | **Remember the quiet dates:** a brief loving note from a ministering sister or RS President on the due date or anniversary means the world—long after others forget. | Ensure Stake *Emotional Resilience* courses and grief counseling referral lists stay current for Bishops and Ward RS Presidencies. |

### 6. Death in the Family / Bereavement
*Governing Handbook Standard:* `GH 29.5` (Funerals conducted under the Bishop's direction; dignified, Christ-centered focus), `GH 38.5.8` (Temple Burial Clothing), `GH 22.4–22.6` (Welfare & Widow/Widower Care).

| Horizon | Individual & Family (First) | Ward & Ministering (Second) | Stake Support (Third) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Preparation (Before)** | Maintain updated will/trust, beneficiaries, life insurance, **Utah Advance Health Care Directive**, and a clear folder (physical + encrypted cloud) listing accounts, passwords, bills, and burial/temple-clothing wishes so surviving family isn't guessing in grief. | Encourage older couples and families during annual financial/document touchpoints to organize key records; maintain strong ministering bonds with elderly and widowed members. | Maintain Stake facilities guidelines (`GH 29.5.4`, `35.5`) for meetinghouse funeral scheduling and keep LDS Family Services grief-group options accessible. |
| **Short-Term (0–72 Hours)** | Contact immediate family, mortuary, and your **Bishop** right away (before locking funeral times so the Bishop can coordinate building/scheduling under `GH 29.5`); lean on trusted family/ward friends to handle phone calls, out-of-town guest logistics, and home coverage. | Bishop meets promptly and lovingly with family to plan a Christ-centered funeral (`GH 29.5.4`) and assign building/AV/music coordination; **Ward RS Presidency & EQ Presidency coordinate together** on family meal/funeral luncheon (respecting family wishes—never overwhelming them), temple burial clothing assistance (`GH 38.5.8` if endowed), home security during the funeral, and yard/snow prep. | Stake Presidency member attends/presides per `GH 29.5.4` when appropriate; High Council Facility Liaison assists with any Building 1 or Building 2 scheduling/overflow logistics if a large stake-wide funeral occurs. |
| **Mid-Term (1–4 Weeks)** | Obtain certified death certificates (typically 8–12 copies); notify Social Security, employer/pension, banks, insurance, and utilities; avoid major rushed financial decisions (selling a home, large investments) during acute grief. | Keep quiet practical care going after out-of-town relatives fly home: trash cans, lawn/snow, meals, rides, and companionship; Bishop privately checks on the surviving spouse's financial transition & fast-offering/Storehouse needs (`GH 22.6.1`); **update confidential Form W-1 (`SN-5` Isolated Senior)** if a spouse now lives alone! | With survivor's consent, connect them to vetted volunteer financial/estate mentors (never sales pitches!) or **LDS Family Services** grief counseling through the Bishop. |
| **Long-Term (2+ Months)** | Rebuild daily rhythms, update your own emergency contact/gathering card and medical directive, and gradually adjust home maintenance & budget for a solo household. | **Months 3–12 are the loneliest:** schedule intentional ministering companionship, Sunday rides, holiday/anniversary remembrance, and ongoing practical home-maintenance buddies (EQ + youth) for widows and widowers. | Sustain Stake *Emotional Resilience* / grief support pathways and keep **Riverview Branch / Saratoga View Senior Living** coordination warm if an aging survivor later transitions to assisted living. |

### 7. Person Getting Married (New Household / Newlyweds — Including YSA)
*Governing Handbook Standard:* `GH 26.3` (Temple Recommend for Living Ordinances), `GH 22.1.4` (Debt-Free Financial Foundations), `GH 21` (Immediate Ministering Integration).

| Horizon | Individual & Family (First) | Ward & Ministering (Second) | Stake Support (Third) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Preparation (Before)** | Schedule Bishop & Stake Presidency interviews early for temple living ordinances (`GH 26.3`); align openly on **finances, debt, budget, and expectations** before the wedding (`GH 22.1.4` — avoid wedding debt!); secure apartment/townhome lease, **renter's insurance**, and health/auto insurance updates (marriage opens a **30–60 day** enrollment window). | Bishopric conducts temple/marriage prep interviews with warmth; YSA/home ward leaders celebrate and guide records transfer smoothly; encourage the couple to attend a *Personal Finances* or marriage-prep class. | Stake Presidency conducts living-ordinance temple recommend interviews (`GH 26.3`); Stake Self-Reliance offers *Personal Finances* & *Emotional Resilience* groups open to engaged/newlywed couples. |
| **Short-Term (0–72 Hours / Week 1)** | Update health/auto/renter's insurance, emergency contacts, and utility accounts; locate your new apartment/townhome **water shut-off and breaker panel** (ask landlord on move-in day); unpack a basic starter flashlight, first-aid kit, and 6 gallons of water (`2 people × 3 days`). | **New Ward:** Ward Clerk moves records promptly upon arrival; Bishopric, EQ & RS Presidencies welcome the couple in their first 2 weeks, assign ministering companions immediately (Form W-4), and share the **Member Prep Guide** (`?tab=8`) with the Renter/Newlywed quick-start box. | Minimal direct Stake role; ensure the **Saratoga Springs SA 11th Ward (YSA)** and all 6 family wards have smooth record-handoff habits so newlyweds aren't "lost between wards" for months. |
| **Mid-Term (1–4 Weeks)** | Complete name-change/ID/Social Security/bank updates (if applicable) and save encrypted scans in cloud backup; build your first shared monthly budget; start a **\$20/week starter pantry** (everyday foods you both eat) + **2-person 72-hr go-bags** + car commuter kits (for crossing the Jordan River to work/school). | Invite the newlyweds to ward activities and callings that help them build real friendships quickly; ministering companions help them feel genuinely known (not treated like invisible renters). | Offer Stake *Personal Finances* course (`GH 22.13`) and invite young couples to optional Stake home-delivered preparedness workshops / Home Storage Center tours. |
| **Long-Term (2+ Months)** | Build steadily toward a **1→3 month emergency fund**, **2 weeks of water (`28 gal` for 2 people — under-bed flat jugs if renting)**, and gradual bulk staples via the **Saratoga Springs Home Storage Center** (`142 W Medical Dr`) using the official Order Form PDF without debt; update wills/beneficiaries. | Include the couple in normal quarterly ministering interviews (`GH 21.3`) and semi-annual kit-rotation reminders (Spring/Fall time change). | Maintain Stake Self-Reliance & Home Storage Center orientation opportunities for young married households. |

### 8. Children Getting Married Locally (Hosting Parents & Empty-Nest Transition)
*Governing Handbook Standard:* `GH 35.5` (Care and Use of Meetinghouses for Receptions — scheduling, decor, music, zero overnight stays, cleanup), `GH 22.1.4` (Avoiding Debt).

| Horizon | Individual & Family (First) | Ward & Ministering (Second) | Stake Support (Third) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Preparation (Before)** | Set a **debt-free wedding & reception budget** (`GH 22.1.4` — don't raid retirement/emergency funds!); reserve the meetinghouse cultural hall early via the Ward Building Scheduler and review **GH 35.5** facility guidelines (no tape/nails damaging walls, modest decor, no alcohol/smoking, cleanup plan); consider gifting the newlyweds practical starter essentials (72-hr backpack, flashlight, fire extinguisher, or a **Home Storage Center** starter case of staples!). | Ward Building Scheduler confirms calendar availability and clearly shares **GH 35.5** setup/cleanup expectations upfront (prevents day-of stress!); ministering friends offer joyful setup/takedown help. | High Council Facility Liaisons ensure clear, consistent Building 1 & Building 2 reception/cleanup guidelines are posted for all Ward Building Schedulers. |
| **Short-Term (0–72 Hours)** | Delegate reception logistics (setup, food refills, teardown, lockup) to trusted friends/relatives so parents can actually be present emotionally; complete thorough meetinghouse cleanup and lock all doors per Building Scheduler checklist. | Ward friends/ministering companions joyfully staff simple kitchen/parking/cleanup shifts so parents aren't scrubbing floors at midnight. | Only involved if multi-ward calendar conflict or facility/AV equipment issue arises at Building 1 or 2. |
| **Mid-Term (1–4 Weeks)** | Reconcile wedding expenses cleanly without lingering debt; update household auto/health insurance (if adult child moves off policies); check in emotionally as a couple—launching a child (especially the last child!) can trigger real **empty-nest grief or identity shift**. | Ministering companions check warmly on parents *after* the wedding excitement fades (especially single parents or widows launching their last child home!). | No special activation; routine Stake spiritual & community fellowship. |
| **Long-Term (2+ Months)** | Recalibrate household water/food storage quantities for fewer people at home (rotate surplus to the newlyweds or donate before expiration!); revisit retirement savings, downsizing, and empty-nest service goals. | Ward Clerk updates household records smoothly; keep empty-nest parents warmly engaged in ministering, temple service, and ward community. | Encourage empty-nest couples/singles to serve as volunteer mentors in Stake Self-Reliance courses (`GH 22.13`) or Senior Service / Preparedness Specialists (`GH 22.9.4`). |

### 9. Divorce or Marital Separation
*Governing Handbook Standard:* `GH 38.6.2` (**Abuse — Zero Tolerance; immediate safety & Church Abuse Help Line `1-800-453-3860`**), `GH 22.4.5` (Strict Confidentiality & Zero Gossip), `GH 22.4–22.6` (Welfare & Self-Reliance), `GH 22.13` (Emotional Resilience & LDS Family Services).

| Horizon | Individual & Family (First) | Ward & Ministering (Second) | Stake Support (Third) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Preparation (Before)** | **If abuse or immediate danger exists:** call `911`, move to a safe location immediately, and notify the Bishop *(Bishops call the 24/7 Church Abuse Help Line per `GH 38.6.2`)*. In any marital strain, seek early counseling (`LDS Family Services` / licensed therapist), keep copies of vital IDs/financial records, and maintain personal credit & employability (`GH 22.1.4`). | Teach healthy Christlike marriage, emotional resilience, and zero tolerance for abuse; cultivate confidential ministering trust so struggling spouses feel safe seeking help early—without fear of gossip. | Keep **LDS Family Services** marriage/family counseling and **Church Abuse Help Line** protocols current with all Bishops (`GH 38.6.2`). |
| **Short-Term (0–72 Hours / Week 1)** | Ensure physical & emotional safety of adults and children first; secure vital personal documents (IDs, birth certificates, bank/insurance records) and immediate housing/living essentials; meet confidentially with your Bishop for spiritual support and urgent temporal triage. | **Strict confidentiality & zero side-taking (`GH 22.4.5`):** Bishop counsels privately with each spouse, calls the Abuse Help Line immediately if any abuse is reported (`GH 38.6.2`), and coordinates urgent fast-offering/housing/food bridge aid (`GH 22.6.1`, Bishops' Storehouse); RS & EQ Presidencies ensure **both** spouses (and children!) have compassionate, discreet ministering companions who never gossip. | Stake President supports the Bishop on complex ecclesiastical, safety, or legal/Abuse Help Line consultations; ensures LDS Family Services counseling referrals are available promptly. |
| **Mid-Term (1–4+ Weeks)** | Consult qualified legal counsel (avoid legal advice from well-meaning neighbors); establish an independent crisis budget, health insurance, and safe custody/school pickup plan; protect children from adult conflict and keep routines predictable. | Quietly update **Primary/Youth leaders** (only what's needed for safe child pickup & extra love); offer practical single-parent help (rides, childcare during legal/job appointments, moving help via EQ); Bishop reviews ongoing Self-Reliance Plan (`GH 22.6.1`) and connects to *Personal Finances* / *Find a Better Job* / *Emotional Resilience*. | Stake Employment Specialist (`GH 22.9.4`) assists a re-entering spouse with resume, career retraining, or **BYU-Pathway / Deseret Industries** job-readiness; Stake Self-Reliance offers welcoming *Emotional Resilience* & *Personal Finances* groups (`GH 22.13`). |
| **Long-Term (2+ Months)** | Rebuild independent financial self-reliance (1→3 month emergency fund, independent insurance, updated will/beneficiaries, solo-household 72-hr kit & emergency contact card); invest in long-term emotional/spiritual healing. | Maintain long-term belonging so divorced/single parents never feel sidelined in a family-heavy Saratoga Springs ward; update **Member Tools** & confidential **Form W-1 (Solo-Parent / Caregiver note)** so they are never missed during storms or emergencies. | Ensure Stake single-adult & family activities are warmly inclusive of single parents and divorced members; maintain ongoing Stake Self-Reliance and counseling pathways. |
