### Segment Slide 1 — *Unified Emergency Plan Overview*
## Scene 1 — Unified Emergency Response & Purpose (`Start Here`)
**On Screen:** Main **Start Here** view spotlighting the 3 connected tiers: Individual & Family Self-Reliance, Ward EQ & RS Care Network, and Stake Coordination & Matching.  
**Narration:**
> Welcome to the Saratoga Springs Utah Crossroads Stake Emergency Plan and Recovery Guide. This offline-ready web app unites families, Wards, and the Stake into one coordinated emergency response — giving everyone clear, decisive steps during an emergency and dependable communication paths that strengthen ministering and recovery. Every family's personal information stays strictly on their own device, with zero cloud accounts, zero passwords, and zero member data ever sent to a server.

### Segment Slide 2 — *The Member Experience (First Hour & Family Prep)*
## Scene 2 — Member Emergency Mode & Red/Yellow/Green Communications (`Members → Emergency Mode`)
**On Screen:** Select **Member** role, spotlight & tap **Emergency Mode**, walk through the **First 60 Minutes** life-safety checklist, then spotlight **GREEN**, **YELLOW**, and **RED** 1-tap check-in buttons on cue, plus FRS walkie-talkie channels.  
**Narration:**
> Let's start with what matters most in a crisis: the Member experience. When an earthquake, winter power outage, or wildfire hits, a member taps Emergency Mode. Instantly, peacetime reading hides away and only the First Hour life-safety steps remain — calling 9-1-1 for life danger, checking gas safely and evacuating upwind if they smell a leak, not flushing toilets until water lines are cleared, and staying off roads until chapels are inspected. Next come clear communication channels from home to Ward. For example: a family that is safe posts Green in their front window or taps Green to text their ministering companion. A senior whose power is out and needs insulin kept cool posts Yellow — signaling a non-life-threatening need for Ward help. And a home with a gas leak, injury, or under two hours of oxygen calls 9-1-1 immediately and posts Red. If cell towers fail, families listen for five minutes at the top of the hour on their Ward's FRS walkie-talkie channel while ministering companions walk the block.

**Narration:**
> Rolling this out across the Stake follows three clear steps shown right on screen. **Narration:**
> Finally, Tab 5 brings together the Church's eleven official disruption categories — from Physical Safety, Shelter, and Medical Services to Power, Water, and Emotional and Spiritual care. Leaders can filter by any single disruption, like Physical Safety, to see every affected hazard at a glance. And when you open any of our seven Saratoga Springs hazard playbooks, like a Wasatch Fault Earthquake, you immediately see all four horizons side by side for households, Wards, and the Stake. Before the event, it outlines practical preparation like strapping water heaters and keeping locked Ward binders ready. Short-Term — hours zero to seventy-two — focuses on immediate life safety, calm presence, and door-to-door ministering checks. Medium-Term — weeks one and two — shifts to sustaining dignity, utilities, host homes, and Bishops' Storehouse coordination. And Long-Term — two months and beyond — guides emotional healing, insurance and community advocacy, and rebuilding family self-reliance. Unified in response, clear in communication, and built for ministering from hour one through full recovery.
