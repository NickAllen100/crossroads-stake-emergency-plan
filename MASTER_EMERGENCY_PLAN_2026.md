# Stake Emergency Operations Plan (Master Binder Edition)

**Jurisdiction:** Saratoga Springs Utah Crossroads Stake  
**Stake Composition:** 7 Wards across 2 Meetinghouse Facilities + **Riverview Branch (Care Center Unit at Saratoga View Senior Living)**  
**Meetinghouse Facilities (2 Buildings):**  
* **Building 1 (Stake Center / Primary Command):** 368 W Aspen Hills Blvd, Saratoga Springs, UT 84045  
* **Building 2 (Harvest Hills Meetinghouse / Secondary Hub):** 270 W Harvest Hills Blvd, Saratoga Springs, UT 84045  
**High-Acuity Residential Care Facility & Ecclesiastical Unit:** Saratoga View Senior Living & Riverview Branch (1429 N Utah Cottage Dr, 385-430-1020)  
**Geographic Perimeter:** Strict 1.0-Mile Radius from Aspen Hills Stake Center  
**Foundational Principle:** **Families start with what they have.** As a volunteer organization operating on limited budgets, emergency response begins with family self-reliance in the home. The Stake and Wards do **not** maintain equipment warehouses or store 55-gallon water barrels at church buildings. Meetinghouse supplies are strictly limited to: (1) handheld FRS walkie-talkie radios in each building library, kept charged; (2) standard meetinghouse first-aid kits; (3) printed emergency binders and laminated radio channel cards; and (4) a small emergency box of N95 masks. All bulk group buys coordinated by the Stake are optional and delivered directly to member homes.  
**Governance Standard:** Directed strictly by the **Stake Presidency**, **High Council**, and **Stake Relief Society Presidency**. Wards operate as autonomous reporting bodies utilizing the standardized [Ward Emergency Assessment & Survey Toolkit](file:///Users/nickallen/code/Emergency%20plan/worksheets/ward_assessment_toolkit.md).  
**Document Approval Status:** Submitted to Stake Presidency for Formal Approval (November 2026)

## 3. Worksheet 2A (Page 6A): Major Earthquake

* **0–72 Hours:** Direct members to shut off home gas meters where odor detected; High Council Facility Liaisons conduct rapid structural check of 368 W Aspen Hills Blvd; fall back to Shay Park Pavilion (516 W Aspen Hills Blvd) if compromised; field medical triage under pop-up canopies; "Do Not Flush" order & twin-bucket toilets; isolate home water heater tanks (close cold-water inlet). Lead: High Council Facility Liaisons, High Council Communications Liaison, Stake Relief Society Presidency, Assigned Wards.
* **1–2 Weeks:** Requisition bulk potable water from Saratoga Springs Bishops' Storehouse (801-436-8205); communal outdoor propane cooking; Stake Relief Society coordinates host-family billeting for red-tagged homes; High Council Logistics coordinates volunteer debris clearing with chainsaws and tractors.
* **2+ Months:** Stake Presidency coordinates rebuilding volunteer support, FEMA/SBA assistance, and LDS Family Services trauma counseling; outdoor consolidated sacrament services.

## 4. Worksheet 2B (Page 6B): WUI Wildfire

* **0–72 Hours:** Wards verify neighborhood evacuation notifications; High Council Care Center Liaison coordinates with Saratoga View Senior Living; volunteer multi-passenger vehicles mobilized for families needing transport; route traffic north on Redwood Rd toward Pioneer Crossing (SR-73); distribute N95/P100 smoke respirators from Stake reserves; off-site check-in via digital/radio rollup. Lead: High Council Public Safety Liaison, High Council Care Center Liaison, Stake Relief Society Presidency, Assigned Wards.
* **1–2 Weeks:** Stake Presidency establishes inter-stake host housing in Lehi and American Fork; clothing/hygiene orders from Saratoga Springs Bishops' Storehouse; police repopulation passes (801-766-6503); indoor smoke remediation using box fans and MERV-13 filters; pre-storm sandbagging around Shay Park drainage gulch.
* **2+ Months:** Post-fire alluvial fan debris flow / flash flood mitigation; total loss housing assistance; 30-foot defensible space compliance days.

## 5. Worksheet 2C (Page 6C): Winter Blizzard & Grid Freeze

* **0–72 Hours:** Establish single insulated "warm room" in homes; strict ban on indoor charcoal grills/generators to prevent carbon monoxide poisoning; Wards conduct twice-daily checks on oxygen users and isolated seniors; open cabinet doors under sinks and trickle exterior faucets to prevent burst pipes; High Council Facility Liaisons activate 368 W Aspen Hills Blvd and 270 W Harvest Hills Blvd as warming shelters. Lead: High Council Facility Liaisons, Stake Relief Society Presidency, High Council Care Center Liaison, Assigned Wards.
* **1–2 Weeks:** High Council Logistics Liaison mobilizes volunteer plumbers with PEX tools and SharkBite fittings to isolate burst pipes during thaw; 4WD snowplowing of church lots and hydrants; hot communal meals at church kitchens using outdoor propane burners under Stake Relief Society direction.
* **2+ Months:** Drywall flood cuts and mold remediation; Church welfare emergency financial relief for insurance deductibles; seminars on home winterization and generator transfer switches.

## 6. Worksheet 2D (Page 6D): High Windstorm & Microburst

* **0–72 Hours:** Strict indoor shelter away from windward windows during 70–90+ mph winds; treat all downed power lines as live 7,200V lines (35-ft perimeter, caution tape, call Rocky Mountain Power 1-877-508-5088); emergency roof tarping with furring strips once winds drop below 25 mph; clear fallen trees from Aspen Hills Blvd. Lead: High Council Logistics Liaison, High Council Facility Liaisons, Stake Relief Society Presidency, Assigned Wards.
* **1–2 Weeks:** Debris haul-off roll-off dumpsters; rebuild fallen boundary fences to protect children and pets from Redwood Rd traffic; inspect church and Riverview Elementary roofs.
* **2+ Months:** Permanent roofing contractor vetting; neighborhood campaign to install 15-inch helical earth auger anchors on all trampolines.

## 7. Worksheet 2E (Page 6E): Hazmat Spill on Redwood Road

* **0–72 Hours:** Mandatory emergency shelter-in-place; **STRICTLY PROHIBIT OUTDOOR TRAVEL OR VEHICULAR EVACUATION** into toxic chemical vapor plume; lock windows/doors, shut off furnace/AC blowers, close fireplace dampers; High Council Public Safety Liaison confirms Riverview Elementary has sealed school; advise parents **NOT** to drive to school; shut off meetinghouse outside-air dampers; decontaminate exposed individuals and call Utah Poison Control (**1-800-222-1222**). Lead: High Council Public Safety Liaison, High Council Facility Liaisons, Stake Relief Society Presidency, Assigned Wards.
* **1–2 Weeks:** Cross-ventilation purge for 30 minutes after official All-Clear; replace home furnace air filters (MERV 11–13); follow up on chemical respiratory irritation with Holy Cross Mountain Point ER (385-345-3000).
* **2+ Months:** Child anxiety debriefing; freight routing advocacy with City/MAG; annual 10-minute shelter-in-place drill.

## 8. Worksheet 2F (Page 6F): Culinary Water System Failure

* **0–72 Hours:** Boil water vigorously for **3 full minutes** (adjusting for 4,500 ft elevation); strict prohibition against consuming untreated secondary irrigation water; Stake Relief Society Presidency coordinates immediate bottled water deliveries to infants on formula and elderly living alone; households isolate home water heaters (40–50 gal on hand); liaison with Public Works Urgent Dispatch (**801-404-2468**). Lead: Stake Relief Society Presidency, High Council Logistics Liaison, High Council Civil Liaison, Assigned Wards.
* **1–2 Weeks:** Stage emergency municipal or Bishops' Storehouse potable water distribution in parking lot of 368 W Aspen Hills Blvd; volunteer handwashing stations with >60% alcohol sanitizer to prevent viral gastrointestinal outbreaks; plumbing line flushing assistance.
* **2+ Months:** Secondary stop-and-waste valve backflow education; optional bulk group buy of food-grade containers and Water Preserver drops **delivered directly to member homes** (zero Church facility storage).

## 9. Worksheet 2G (Page 6G): Widespread Disease Outbreak & Epidemic / Pandemic

* **0–72 Hours:** Active monitoring & civil health liaison; High Council Public Safety Liaison establishes communications with Utah County Health Department (801-851-7000); review Stake assembly protocols (temporary transition to virtual meetings / sacrament in homes if mandated by Church and civil authorities); Wards activate contactless ministering check-ins via phone/SMS (no call trees); strict messaging that Church facilities are **NOT** medical clinics and do not store medicines; educate households to rely on home care supplies (fluids, fever reducers, pulse oximeters). Lead: Stake Presidency, High Council Public Safety Liaison, Stake Relief Society Presidency, Assigned Wards.
* **1–2 Weeks:** Coordinate contactless doorstep delivery of groceries, OTC medications, and hydration fluids to isolated/quarantined families; coordinate with local Bishops on Saratoga Springs Bishops' Storehouse commodity orders for wage earners experiencing quarantine income loss; High Council Care Center Liaison coordinates zero-contact perimeter/pastoral check-ins with Saratoga View Senior Living nursing administration.
* **2+ Months:** Post-surge emotional and bereavement ministering led by Stake Relief Society; family self-reliance re-stocking workshops (maintaining 90-day supply of OTC medications, masks, soap, and non-perishables); facilitate volunteer blood drives at meetinghouses in partnership with ARUP or American Red Cross once facilities are cleared.
