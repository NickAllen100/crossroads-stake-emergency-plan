## 1. Executive Summary & Self-Reliance Framework

This Emergency Operations Plan establishes a coordinated, life-safety preparedness framework for all Church members and neighborhood residents residing across the **7 Wards of the Stake meeting in our two primary meetinghouses: 368 W Aspen Hills Blvd (Stake Center) and 270 W Harvest Hills Blvd**, alongside the **Riverview Branch at Saratoga View Senior Living**.

Rather than an abstract checklist, this plan addresses the **top 7 documented hazards** affecting our hillside location between Lake Mountain and Redwood Road, utilizing verified municipal data from Saratoga Springs Fire & Rescue, Police, Emergency Management, and Alpine School District.

### Core Philosophy: Family Self-Reliance & Volunteer Reality
* **All Volunteers, Limited Budgets:** Our organization is staffed entirely by volunteers operating on limited budgets. Emergency response begins with individual and family self-reliance: **"Families start with what they have."**
* **Zero Church Warehouse Storage:** The Stake and Wards do **not** maintain warehouses or storage space for items like 55-gallon drums, physical bulk supplies, or heavy machinery. Any bulk preparedness drives coordinated by the Stake are **optional group buys delivered directly to member homes**.
* **Minimal Meetinghouse On-Site Gear:** Physical equipment kept on-site at meetinghouses is strictly limited to:
  1. Handheld FRS walkie-talkie radios stored and kept charged in each building library.
  2. Standard meetinghouse first-aid kits already on hand.
  3. Printed emergency operations binders and laminated radio frequency cards.
  4. A small emergency box of N95 masks.
* **Two-Tier Governance Model:**
  1. **Stake Command & Rollup Tier:** Strictly governed by the **Stake Presidency**, **High Council**, and **Stake Relief Society Presidency**, focusing on facilities, communications, anonymized special needs rollups, voluntary equipment sharing registers, and inter-agency coordination.
  2. **Ward Assessment & Survey Toolkit:** A standardized instrument distributed to the 7 Wards containing structured blank survey sheets for local confidential intake (Form W-1), voluntary equipment tracking (Form W-2), medical skills (Form W-3), neighborhood accountability (Form W-4), and anonymized reporting slips (Form W-5).

## 2. Key Operational Vulnerabilities & Lifelines

### A. The Jordan River Island Isolation Risk (Earthquake Scenario)
* In a Major Wasatch Fault Earthquake (M7.0+), soil liquefaction along the Jordan River/Utah Lake will compromise bridge approaches on Pioneer Crossing (SR-73) and 2100 North, **cutting off road access to Lehi hospitals and I-15**.
* **Critical Finding:** Our plan designates **Intermountain Saratoga Springs Emergency Services (392 W Medical Dr, 801-763-3500)** as our primary trauma center because it is located **on our side of the river directly in Saratoga Springs**, eliminating the bridge failure hazard.
* Northbound corridor traffic will utilize **Westlake Emergency Center (700 N Redwood Rd, 385-338-2600)**, an open 24/7 freestanding ER just 3.5 miles north.

### B. Institutional Special Needs: Saratoga View Senior Living & Riverview Branch
* Located within our stake boundary at **1429 N Utah Cottage Dr (Phone: 385-430-1020)**, this facility houses 74–100 senior residents with memory care/dementia and assisted living needs.
* **On-Site Pastoral Unit:** The Church maintains the **Riverview Branch** directly within this facility to minister to residents.
* **High Council Care Center Liaison:** A designated member of the High Council is assigned to maintain ongoing coordination with the facility Executive Director and Director of Nursing. In a wind-driven wildfire or sub-zero power freeze, the Stake coordinates volunteer labor to assist nursing staff with wheelchair transfers, memory care wander prevention along perimeter fence lines, and auxiliary emergency warming *strictly under direction of facility staff with zero Church medical liability*.

### C. Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) Wildfire (Lake Mountain)
* As demonstrated by the 2020 Knolls Fire (which evacuated 13,000 Saratoga Springs residents), dry cheatgrass on Lake Mountain slopes can reach homes in under 45 minutes during high-wind events.
* Our plan provides Wards with structured neighborhood zone mapping tools to ensure rapid household verification for elderly and non-ambulatory members.

### D. Riverview Elementary Daytime Reunification
* **Riverview Elementary School (273 W Aspen Hills Blvd, 385-579-7355)** is directly adjacent to the Stake Center. Over 70% of neighborhood parents commute to Salt Lake or Utah counties during the day.
* In a daytime earthquake, wildfire, or Redwood Road hazardous chemical spill, the Stake Center parking lot and grounds will serve as the **Official Parent-Student Reunification Staging Zone** in direct partnership with the school principal.

## 3. Dual-Hub Facilities & Communications Strategy

* **Dual Meetinghouse Hubs:**
  * **Hub 1 (South Sector / Stake Center):** 368 W Aspen Hills Blvd (Command Post, Triage, School Liaison).
  * **Hub 2 (North Sector / Harvest Hills):** 270 W Harvest Hills Blvd (Northern Sector Shelter, Senior Care Liaison).
* **Amateur Radio (HAM) Net:** Verified regional links on **Utah County ARES (147.340 MHz)** and **Lake Mountain North (145.230 MHz)** for Stake-to-Church Welfare communication.
* **Building Library Walkie-Talkies (FRS Handhelds):** Kept charged in Building 1 & 2 libraries for on-site facility liaisons, parking sentries, and shelter coordinators.
* **Neighborhood Walkie-Talkies (FRS/GMRS):** Because 95% of families do not hold a HAM license, we have established a 3-channel plan open to all families without a license:
  * **Channel 1:** General Ward Emergency Check-In
  * **Channel 3:** Evacuation Logistics & Sector Assessment
  * **Channel 5:** Welfare, Medical Care & Vulnerable Needs
