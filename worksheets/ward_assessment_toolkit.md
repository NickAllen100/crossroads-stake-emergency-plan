# Ward Emergency Assessment & Survey Toolkit
### Saratoga Springs Utah Crossroads Stake

**Distribution:** For immediate delivery to each Ward within the Crossroads Stake  
**Purpose:** Standardized intake instruments for Wards to survey member special needs, critical emergency equipment, specialized medical/technical skills, and neighborhood communication nets, and transmit anonymized rollups to the Stake Presidency, High Council, and Stake Relief Society Presidency.  
**Governing Standard:** Church Welfare Services *Stake and Ward Emergency Planning Guide*  
**Foundational Principle:** **Families start with what they have.** As a volunteer organization operating on limited budgets, emergency preparedness begins in the home. The Stake and Wards do **not** maintain storage space or warehouses for 55-gallon barrels, bulk supplies, or heavy machinery. Meetinghouse on-site supplies are strictly minimal: library walkie-talkie (FRS) radios (kept charged), first-aid kits, printed binders, and a small box of N95 masks.

---

## Instructions for Ward Leadership

1. **Family Self-Reliance First:** Distribute the companion [Ward Member & Family Preparedness Guide](file:///Users/nickallen/code/Emergency%20plan/worksheets/ward_member_preparedness_guide.md) (Tab 9 of the presentation) to every household so families prepare 72-hour kits, 2 weeks of water & food, and auxiliary medical power before asking the Ward to fill gaps. Bulk orders organized by the Stake are optional group buys delivered directly to member homes (zero Church building storage).
2. **Confidentiality:** Personal medical information and member contact details collected on **Form W-1** are strictly confidential. Form W-1 remains within the local Ward emergency binder and is **never** published publicly or transmitted outside local leadership.
3. **Ministering Accountability (No Call Trees):** Wards do **not** use fragile phone/call trees. Use **Form W-4** to organize household check-ins through existing **Elders Quorum & Relief Society Ministering Districts** paired with immediate neighbor buddies for in-person checks when networks fail.
4. **Anonymized Stake Rollup:** Wards do not send individual member names to the Stake. Instead, Wards complete **Form W-5 (Ward-to-Stake Anonymized Transmission Slip)**, reporting only summary numbers, category counts, and critical unmet mutual-aid needs to the **Stake Relief Society Presidency** and assigned **High Council Liaison**.
5. **Annual Audit:** Each Ward conducts a comprehensive update of these survey forms annually during Q1 and immediately updates local records upon new member move-ins.

## Ward Leader Roadmap: How to Teach, Build & Maintain Your Ward Plan

### Part A: First 90 Days — Initial Ward Launch Checklist
* [ ] **1. Align in Ward Council:** Walk through Tab 1 (Three-Tier Model & 5 Gathering Locations including Shay Park) and agree on how EQ & RS will run Ministering check-ins.
* [ ] **2. Map Ministering Accountability (Form W-4):** Confirm every household is assigned to a Ministering District, note immediate neighbor buddies for non-member/isolated homes, and record backup runners/FRS channels.
* [ ] **3. Share the Member Prep Guide (Tab 9):** Distribute the handout to every household and invite members to update their phone/address in the **Church Member Tools** app.
* [ ] **4. Conduct Confidential Special Needs & Aux Power Check (Form W-1):** Through quiet ministering or RS/EQ outreach, identify SN-1 through SN-8 households and verify whether power-dependent members **already have auxiliary power**.
* [ ] **5. Gather Voluntary Skills & Gear (Forms W-2 & W-3):** Invite willing members to share medical licenses, trades, HAM callsigns, portable power stations, chainsaws, or gravity filters for neighbor-to-neighbor help.
* [ ] **6. Submit Anonymized Rollup (Form W-5):** Send numerical counts and unmet needs (zero names) to Stake leadership so Stake Registers stay accurate.
* [ ] **7. Stage One Local Ward Binder:** Keep printed Forms W-1–W-5 and laminated HAM/FRS cards in the Bishop's office for offline access.

### Part B: Annual 4-Quarter Teaching & Maintenance Calendar (Repeat Yearly)
* **Q1 (Jan–Mar) — Water, Food & Annual Audit:** Teach *Home Storage & Water* (*Utah Area Guide* pp. 11–17; highlight **Saratoga Springs Home Storage Center**, 142 W Medical Dr, Suite 1 • 801-436-8205). Refresh Forms W-1–W-4 for move-ins and submit updated **Form W-5** by March 31.
* **Q2 (Apr–Jun) — 72-Hr Kits, Wildfire & Gathering Plans:** Teach *Emergency Kits & Communication Plans* (*Utah Guide* pp. 6–10) — 15-min wildfire go-bags, Everbridge alerts, out-of-town contacts, Shay Park & Riverview Elementary. Run spring Daylight Saving Time reminder + quick **15-min Ministering reachability check**.
* **Q3 (Jul–Sep) — Home Safety, Utility Shut-Offs & Earthquake:** Teach *Preparing Your Home* (*Utah Guide* p. 5) — gas/water/water-heater shut-offs, twin-bucket toilets, trampoline anchoring, Hazmat shelter-in-place. September Preparedness Month: encourage a 10-min family drill and test Ward FRS Ch 1/3/5 with HAM liaison.
* **Q4 (Oct–Dec) — Winter Freeze, Aux Power & Resilience:** Teach *Financial & Emotional Preparedness* (*Utah Guide* pp. 18–25) + winter warm-room & CO safety. Fall Daylight Saving Time check: Ministering companions quietly verify SN-1 **auxiliary medical power**, isolated seniors, and snow-shoveling buddies.

**Check-In Protocol:**
1. Secure your own family and home first.
2. Check on your assigned **Ministering households** and immediate next-door neighbors (on foot if phones/power are out; phone/text only during Hazmat plumes or contagious quarantine).
