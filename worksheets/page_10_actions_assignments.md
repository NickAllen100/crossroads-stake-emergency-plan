## 2. Recommended Practice & Maintenance Frequency (Personal • Ward • Stake)

* **Personal & Family:**
  * **Twice a Year (Spring & Fall — Daylight Saving Time / General Conference):** Check 72-hr kits (outgrown clothes, expired food/meds), test flashlights/NOAA radio & **auxiliary medical battery packs**, refresh stored water, and verify Member Tools contact info.
  * **Once a Year (10-Min Family Drill):** Pick one skill—walk to gas/water shut-offs, run Drop-Cover-Hold (April Great Utah ShakeOut), or time a 15-minute wildfire go-bag grab.
  * **Weekly / Monthly Habit:** Add 1–2 extra shelf-stable foods you already eat to normal shopping; build gradually toward a 1-month emergency fund.
* **Ward & Ministering:**
  * **Quarterly (4-Quarter Ward Roadmap in [ward_assessment_toolkit.md](file:///Users/nickallen/code/Emergency%20plan/worksheets/ward_assessment_toolkit.md)):** Q1 Annual Audit & Water/Food Lesson; Q2 72-Hr Kits, Wildfire & Spring Reachability Check; Q3 Home Shut-Offs & Preparedness Month; Q4 Winter Freeze, Aux Medical Power & Financial/Emotional Resilience.
  * **Twice a Year (15-Min Accountability Practice):** Low-stress ministering reachability check (no call trees).
  * **Ongoing (Every Move-In):** Assign new families to a Ministering District (Form W-4) and gently ask about confidential special needs (Form W-1).
* **Stake Council:**
  * **Quarterly (Every 60–90 Days):** Verify Building 1 & 2 library FRS walkie-talkie charging + brief 15-minute Stake HAM check-in net.
  * **Semi-Annually (2× / Year):** Touch base with Saratoga View Senior Living nursing leadership; verify building binders and N95 box.
  * **Annually (Q1 Rollup + Fall Drill):** Refresh Tabs 4 & 5 from Ward Form W-5 returns, confirm City/School/Saratoga Springs Bishops' Storehouse contacts, and run one 60-minute Stake Council tabletop exercise.
