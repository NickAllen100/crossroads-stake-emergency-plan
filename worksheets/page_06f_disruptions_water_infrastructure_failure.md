# Worksheet 2F (Page 6F): Planning for Disruptions — Water System Failure

**Disaster:** Culinary Water Infrastructure Rupture & Cross-Contamination  
**Focal Location:** Within 1.0 Mile of 368 W Aspen Hills Blvd, Saratoga Springs, UT 84045  
**Primary Impact:** Severe transmission main rupture or booster pump outage cutting off potable tap water to hillside pressure zones, or backflow cross-contamination introducing untreated secondary irrigation water into the drinking water system, resulting in a mandatory City-wide Boil Water Order and acute sanitation shortages.  
**Foundational Principle:** **Families start with what they have.** Water preparedness begins in the home using pitchers, clean pots, household water heaters (40–50 gallons on hand), and existing containers. The Stake and Wards do **not** have storage space for 55-gallon barrels or pallets at meetinghouse facilities. Any bulk water containers coordinated by the Stake are organized as **optional group buys delivered directly to member homes**.  
**Governance Standard:** Directed strictly by the **Stake Presidency**, **High Council**, and **Stake Relief Society Presidency**. Wards execute local clean water verification and report to the Stake Center.
