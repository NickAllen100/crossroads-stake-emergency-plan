# Worksheet 2E (Page 6E): Planning for Disruptions — Hazmat Toxic Plume Spill

**Disaster:** Hazardous Materials Tanker Spill on SR-68 (Redwood Road Corridor)  
**Focal Location:** Within 1.0 Mile of 368 W Aspen Hills Blvd, Saratoga Springs, UT 84045  
**Primary Impact:** High-density commercial tanker collision or valve rupture releasing toxic chemical vapor (e.g., chlorine gas, anhydrous ammonia, volatile petrochemicals) into the atmosphere within 0.5 miles of neighborhood residences, triggering emergency shelter-in-place orders, total road closure of SR-68, and acute respiratory toxicity hazards.  
**Foundational Principle:** **Families start with what they have.** Shelter-in-place safety relies on immediate home sealing using household supplies (plastic, towels, duct tape). Meetinghouses do not store commercial air filtration units or bulk PPE warehouses; on-site supplies are strictly limited to library walkie-talkie (FRS) radios, first-aid kits, printed binders, and a small box of N95 masks.  
**Governance Standard:** Directed strictly by the **Stake Presidency**, **High Council**, and **Stake Relief Society Presidency**. Wards execute local shelter-in-place verification and report to the Stake Center.
