# Worksheet 4 (Page 8): Stake Resource Matching & Communications Register

**Stake:** Saratoga Springs Utah Crossroads Stake  
**Core Role:** **Connecting unmet needs to real local resources.** Aside from our two meetinghouses (and minimal library FRS radios, first-aid kits, binders, and one N95 box), the Stake does not warehouse gear or food. Our real capacity is connecting unmet needs reported by Wards (Form W-5) to willing member skills/gear, safe building spaces, and local Saratoga Springs lifelines.

---

## 1. Live "Need → Resource" Matching Playbook

| Unmet Need Reported (Form W-5) | Step 1: Ward / Neighbor Match | Step 2: Stake Cross-Ward / Facility Match | Step 3: External / Civil Escalation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SN-1 Power Running Out** *(Oxygen, CPAP, medical device)* | Borrow nearby member's portable lithium power station (Form W-2) or inverter generator. | Match a 1000Wh+ battery station from an adjacent Ward; or recharge at a powered meetinghouse hub (per FM guidelines). | Call **Saratoga Springs Fire (801-766-6505 / 911)** for priority transport to Intermountain ER. |
| **SN-2 Insulin / Meds Warming** *(Outage >4 hrs in heat)* | Place in neighbor's generator-backed fridge or home cooler with gel packs. | Stake Relief Society opens labeled cooler/ice station in Building 1 or 2 kitchen using member-loaned coolers. | Coordinate with local pharmacy or **Intermountain Saratoga Springs ER (801-763-3500)**. |
| **SN-3 Evacuation / Lift Help** *(Wheelchair / Saratoga View)* | Pre-assigned ministering companion or neighbor with accessible van/SUV. | Dispatch cross-ward volunteer lift pairs (at Saratoga View, strictly under facility nurse direction). | Request paratransit / Fire EMS via **City Emergency Mgmt (801-766-9793 Ext. 219)**. |
| **No Safe Water / Infant Formula (SN-7)** | Share neighbor stored water, isolated 40–50 gal water heater, or gravity filter (Form W-2). | Stake Presidency & Bishops request emergency canned water & formula from **Saratoga Springs Bishops' Storehouse (142 W Medical Dr • 801-436-8205)**; stage at Building 1 lot. | Coordinate City water trailer via **Public Works Urgent Line (801-404-2468)**. |
| **Displaced, Red-Tagged, or Freezing Home** | Short-term stay with intact/heated neighbor home in the same Ward. | Stake Relief Society matches cross-ward host families; open Building 1/2 (if structurally cleared & heated) or Shay Park tents. | Coordinate regional sheltering with **American Red Cross (801-373-8580)** or neighboring Stakes. |
| **Blocked Street, Burst Pipe, or Roof Breach** | Ward member chainsaw crew (with PPE), plumber (PEX/SharkBite), or tarp team (Forms W-2/W-3). | Stake brokers cross-ward skilled trades (electricians, plumbers, 4WD snowplows, trailers) to priority streets & elderly homes. | Report arterial blockages/leaks/wires to **Public Works, Enbridge Gas (1-800-767-1689), or RMP (1-877-508-5088)**. |
| **Trauma / Medical Surge** | Good Samaritan stabilization by Ward MD/RN/EMT volunteer (Form W-3) using first-aid kits. | Open outdoor triage canopy at Building 1/2 parking lot or **Shay Park Pavilion**; keep quiet classroom for SN-6 sensory needs. | Transport immediately to **Intermountain Saratoga Springs ER (392 W Medical Dr)** — west of Jordan River. |
