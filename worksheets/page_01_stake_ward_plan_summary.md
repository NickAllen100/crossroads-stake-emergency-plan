# Stake Emergency Operations Plan — Summary & Self-Reliance Framework

**Stake:** Saratoga Springs Utah Crossroads Stake  
**Stake Composition:** 7 Wards across 2 Meetinghouse Facilities + **Riverview Branch (Care Center Unit at Saratoga View Senior Living)**  
**Geographic Perimeter:** 1.0-Mile Radius from Stake Centers (Covering Aspen Hills & Harvest Hills)  
**Date of Plan:** November 2026  
**Plan Version:** 2026.4 (Self-Reliance & Volunteer Edition)  

---

## Foundational Principle: Family Self-Reliance

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Families Start with What They Have**  
> As a volunteer organization operating on limited budgets, our emergency response begins in the home. Each household is encouraged to practice self-reliance and build personal resilience starting with their own everyday resources (stored pantry items, home first-aid supplies, camping gear, and water containers).  
> The Stake and Wards do **not** maintain physical warehouses or long-term storage facilities for large equipment, fuel, or 55-gallon water barrels. Physical items kept on site at meetinghouses are strictly limited to:
> 1. Emergency walkie-talkie (FRS) radios in each building library, kept charged.
> 2. Standard meetinghouse first-aid kits already on hand.
> 3. Printed emergency plan binders and laminated radio frequency pocket cards.
> 4. A small emergency box of N95 masks.  
> Any bulk equipment or preparedness drives coordinated by the Stake are organized strictly as **optional group buys delivered directly to member homes**, with zero Church facility storage.

## Leadership Chain of Command (Stake Level)

1. **Stake Presidency:** Overall direction, ecclesiastical authority, Area Seventy liaison, emergency declarations. Approves emergency welfare commodity orders drawn on the Saratoga Springs Bishops' Storehouse in consultation with local Bishops.
2. **High Council (Sector & Functional Liaisons):**
   * **Facility Liaison — Building 1 (Stake Center):** On-site command post, shelter operations, generator/utility readiness.
   * **Facility Liaison — Building 2 (Harvest Hills):** Northern shelter staging, auxiliary logistics, facility readiness.
   * **Care Center Liaison — Saratoga View Senior Living & Riverview Branch:** External mutual-aid liaison with facility administration and nursing; volunteer evacuation manpower under nurse direction.
   * **Communications & Logistics Liaison:** Stake Amateur Radio (HAM) net operations, county/UCARES liaison, inter-ward resource sharing.
   * **Public Safety & Civil Liaison:** Interface with Saratoga Springs Fire, Police, Public Works, and Riverview Elementary administration.
3. **Stake Relief Society Presidency:** Welfare & humanitarian oversight, shelter comfort/food logistics, emotional/spiritual support, anonymized special needs rollup aggregation.
4. **The Wards (Autonomous Reporting Units):** 7 Wards + Riverview Branch conduct local surveys using the standardized Ward Toolkit and transmit Form W-5 anonymized rollups to Stake leadership.
