# Worksheet 2B (Page 6B): Planning for Disruptions — WUI Wildfire

**Disaster:** Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) Wildfire (Lake Mountain Slope Fire / High Wind Spread)  
**Focal Location:** Within 1.0 Mile of 368 W Aspen Hills Blvd, Saratoga Springs, UT 84045  
**Primary Impact:** Rapid flame front advancing from dry cheatgrass/sagebrush on Lake Mountain slopes, heavy dense toxic smoke, mandatory evacuation order for 3,000+ homes, gridlock on Redwood Road (SR-68), electrical de-energization by Rocky Mountain Power, and threat of structural loss.  
**Foundational Principle:** **Families start with what they have.** Households maintain their own 72-hour grab-and-go kits, medications, and vehicle fuel. Meetinghouses maintain no equipment warehouses; on-site supplies are strictly limited to library walkie-talkie (FRS) radios, first-aid kits, printed binders, and a small box of N95 masks.  
**Governance Standard:** Directed strictly by the **Stake Presidency**, **High Council**, and **Stake Relief Society Presidency**. Wards execute local neighborhood evacuation verification and report to the Stake.
