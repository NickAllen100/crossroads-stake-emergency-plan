# Worksheet 2D (Page 6D): Planning for Disruptions — High Windstorm & Microburst

**Disaster:** Severe High Windstorm & Downslope Microburst (70–90+ mph Gusts)  
**Focal Location:** Within 1.0 Mile of 368 W Aspen Hills Blvd, Saratoga Springs, UT 84045  
**Primary Impact:** Severe downslope gusts off Lake Mountain/Traverse Mountains tearing off roof shingles, blowing out fences and vinyl siding, lofting unanchored trampolines and sheds into power lines, downing electric poles, and causing localized power outages and blinding dust.  
**Foundational Principle:** **Families start with what they have.** Home resilience begins with securing personal property, anchoring trampolines, and utilizing personal tools. Meetinghouses do not store construction equipment or dumpsters; on-site supplies are strictly limited to library walkie-talkie (FRS) radios, first-aid kits, printed binders, and N95 masks.  
**Governance Standard:** Directed strictly by the **Stake Presidency**, **High Council**, and **Stake Relief Society Presidency**. Wards mobilize local volunteer pairs and report operational status to the Stake Center.
