# Worksheet 2C (Page 6C): Planning for Disruptions — Winter Blizzard & Grid Freeze

**Disaster:** Severe Winter Blizzard & Extended Power Grid Freeze  
**Focal Location:** Within 1.0 Mile of 368 W Aspen Hills Blvd, Saratoga Springs, UT 84045  
**Primary Impact:** Sub-zero temperatures (-10°F to 10°F), heavy drifting snow making roads impassable, multi-day electric grid blackout shutting down residential furnaces, widespread burst culinary water pipes, acute hypothermia risk, and carbon monoxide poisoning threats.  
**Foundational Principle:** **Families start with what they have.** Cold-weather resilience begins in the home with winter clothing, blankets, and safe indoor heating alternatives. The Stake and Wards maintain no warehouse storage for generators, heaters, or fuel; on-site meetinghouse gear is strictly limited to library walkie-talkie (FRS) radios, first-aid kits, printed binders, and N95 masks.  
**Governance Standard:** Directed strictly by the **Stake Presidency**, **High Council**, and **Stake Relief Society Presidency**. Wards execute local neighborhood warming verification and report to the Stake Center.
