# Worksheet 3 (Page 7): Critical Information — Stake Special Needs Master Rollup & Support Matrix

**Stake:** Saratoga Springs Utah Crossroads Stake  
**Planning Standard:** Handbook 1 / General Handbook (Welfare & Preparedness) — Two-Tier Governance  
**Scope:** 7 Wards across 2 Meetinghouse Facilities + Riverview Branch at Saratoga View Senior Living  
**Foundational Principle:** **Families start with what they have.** Home resilience begins with family self-reliance and voluntary neighbor-to-neighbor sharing. The Stake and Wards do **not** maintain warehouses or storage facilities for medical hardware, oxygen tanks, or supplies. On-site church building gear is strictly limited to library walkie-talkie (FRS) radios (kept charged), first-aid kits, printed binders, and a small box of N95 masks.  
**Data Privacy & Confidentiality Notice:**  
Personal member names, physical addresses, and specific medical diagnoses are strictly confidential and protected under Church privacy guidelines. Detailed individual surveys are conducted and secured exclusively within local Ward emergency binders using **[Form W-1 (Ward Special Needs Survey)](file:///Users/nickallen/code/Emergency%20plan/worksheets/ward_assessment_toolkit.md)**.  
The Stake Plan maintains **only an anonymized numerical rollup** and capability support register. The **Stake Relief Society Presidency** aggregates these numbers from Ward **Form W-5** submissions to identify aggregate vulnerable populations and coordinate mutual-aid or Bishops' Storehouse requisitions.

## 3. Stake Capability Support & Mutual-Aid Triggers

As a volunteer organization practicing self-reliance, **families start with what they have** in home supplies, backup battery packs, and private arrangements. When verified ward numbers exceed immediate family capacities, the following Stake support protocols activate:

1. **Electrical Life Support Support (Oxygen / CPAP):**
   * *Circumstance:* Oxygen-dependent individuals reported across the Stake without functioning home battery backup during a power outage exceeding 2 hours.
   * *Action:* **Stake Presidency** and **High Council Logistics Liaison** coordinate with members who own portable battery power stations or invite residents to recharge at the meetinghouse warming station, or contact Saratoga Springs Fire Station 251 (801-766-6505) for priority medical transport.
2. **Insulin Cold-Chain Preservation:**
   * *Circumstance:* Power outage exceeds 4 hours during summer ambient temperatures (>90°F).
   * *Action:* **Stake Relief Society Presidency** opens meetinghouse kitchen cooler storage at 368 W Aspen Hills and 270 W Harvest Hills, pooling member-donated coolers and ice packs to consolidate member medication bags labeled with private member codes.
3. **Jordan River Bridge Severance (Dialysis & Critical Care):**
   * *Circumstance:* SR-73 (Pioneer Crossing) or 2100 North bridges compromised by earthquake liquefaction.
   * *Action:* Critical medical traffic re-routed strictly westward to **Intermountain Saratoga Springs Emergency Services (392 W Medical Dr, 801-763-3500)**; **High Council Civil Liaison** coordinates emergency helicopter LZ at Shay Park (516 W Aspen Hills Blvd) with Utah County Sheriff Dispatch.

## 4. Institutional Care Protocol: Saratoga View Senior Living & Riverview Branch

* **Facility Profile:**
  * **Name:** Saratoga View Senior Living (Assisted Living & Memory Care)
  * **Ecclesiastical Unit:** **Riverview Branch** (Branch organized to minister to resident members within the facility)
  * **Location:** 1429 N Utah Cottage Dr, Saratoga Springs, UT 84045 *(~0.7 mi NE of Stake Center)*
  * **Phone:** (385) 430-1020
  * **Capacity:** 74–100 licensed resident beds (includes dedicated Alzheimer's and memory care secure wing).
* **Legal Boundary & Operational Mandate:**
  * Saratoga View Senior Living is a private, state-regulated healthcare facility with licensed nursing staff. The Church and Stake **do not** assume medical liability, diagnostic responsibility, or facility command.
  * The Stake's role is strictly **External Mutual-Aid, Pastoral Support, and Volunteer Evacuation Manpower**.
* **Designated Stake Leadership Assignment:**
  * **High Council Care Center Liaison:** Designated member of the High Council assigned specifically to maintain ongoing liaison with the Saratoga View Executive Director and Director of Nursing.
  * *Cadence:* Semi-annual planning meeting to review emergency access, volunteer credentials, and perimeter transfer protocols.
* **Emergency Response Actions Under Facility Direction:**
  1. **Memory Care Perimeter Security:** In an earthquake or fire alarm where facility magnetic doors release, disorientation poses an acute wandering hazard into smoke or Redwood Road traffic. High Council Care Center Liaison mobilizes volunteer adult sentries stationed at exterior fence lines to assist staff in keeping residents within safe zones.
  2. **Non-Ambulatory Evacuation Surge:** If full evacuation is ordered by Saratoga Springs Fire, the facility's shift staff cannot move dozens of wheelchairs down corridors simultaneously. High Council Liaison coordinates volunteer pairs to report to the facility entrance to wheel residents to transport buses *strictly under direction of facility nurses*.
  3. **Auxiliary Utility Failure Support:** If the facility auxiliary generator fails during sub-zero temperatures, the Stake Presidency coordinates emergency warming shelter staging at the 270 W Harvest Hills Blvd meetinghouse (~0.8 mi away).
