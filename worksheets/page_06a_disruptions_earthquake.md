# Worksheet 2A (Page 6A): Planning for Disruptions — Major Earthquake

**Disaster:** Major Earthquake (Wasatch Fault Zone / Utah Lake Segment M7.0+)  
**Focal Location:** Within 1.0 Mile of 368 W Aspen Hills Blvd, Saratoga Springs, UT 84045  
**Primary Impact:** Severe structural damage, ruptured natural gas mains, severed culinary water and sewer pipes, bridge approach liquefaction cutting off access to Lehi/I-15, and total electric grid failure.  
**Foundational Principle:** **Families start with what they have.** Volunteer response relies on home self-reliance and voluntary neighbor-to-neighbor sharing. The Stake and Wards maintain no warehouse storage for bulk supplies or 55-gallon drums; meetinghouse supplies are strictly limited to library walkie-talkie (FRS) radios (kept charged), first-aid kits, printed binders, and N95 masks.  
**Governance Standard:** Strategic command directed strictly by **Stake Presidency**, **High Council**, and **Stake Relief Society Presidency**. Wards mobilize local volunteer pairs and report operational status to the Stake Center command post.
