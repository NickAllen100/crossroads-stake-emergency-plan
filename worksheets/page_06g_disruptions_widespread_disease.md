# Worksheet 2G (Page 6G): Planning for Disruptions — Widespread Disease Outbreak & Epidemic / Pandemic

**Focus Areas:** Household isolation ("sick rooms"), contactless doorstep food & prescription care, pulse oximeter loaner program, care facility shielding, and Saratoga Springs Bishops' Storehouse food commodities.  
**Leadership Scope:** Stake Presidency, High Council Care Center Liaison, Stake Relief Society Presidency, Assigned Wards.  
**Foundational Philosophy:** *Families start with what they have.* The Church does not operate medical clinics or store pharmaceuticals.

---

## 1. Hazard Scenario & Categories of Concern

* **Scenario Description:** High-virulence respiratory epidemic, novel viral pathogen, or severe infectious cluster spreading rapidly through suburban neighborhoods. In a community with large family sizes (average 4.2+ individuals per home) and a dedicated senior care center (Saratoga View Senior Living / Riverview Branch), illness spreads rapidly through households. Outpatient clinics and regional emergency departments face severe bed saturation and staffing shortages. In-person church services and public gatherings are suspended or strictly modified upon Area Seventy and Stake Presidency direction.
* **Categories of Concern:**
  * **Human Vulnerability & Life Safety:** High fever, severe respiratory distress, acute complications for frail elderly residents and immunocompromised members; critical prescription access.
  * **Structural & Property Hazards:** Aerosol and surface contamination in homes and meetinghouse facilities; need for dedicated home sick rooms, cross-ventilation, and professional facility terminal sanitization.
  * **Community Infrastructure & Logistics:** Riverview Elementary school closures; acute absenteeism among first responders, healthcare staff, and municipal utility workers; restrictions on public transportation.
  * **Supply Chain & Economic Continuity:** Quarantined families unable to procure groceries or pharmacy items; financial distress for hourly wage earners; severe regional shortages of fever reducers and electrolytes.
* **Church Disruption Categories Impacted (8 / 11):** Food/Cooking, Physical Safety, Shelter/Clothing, Church Facilities, Communication, Emotional/Spiritual, Medical Services, Transportation/Delivery.

---

## 2. Response Across Three Time Horizons

### Short-Term Needs (0–72 Hours): Immediate Infection Control & Care
1. **Home Isolation & "Sick Room" Directive:** Direct households with symptomatic individuals to immediately isolate the sick member in a dedicated bedroom with exclusive bathroom use if possible, open windows for fresh air ventilation, and designate a single healthy caregiver wearing an N95 mask.
2. **Worship Schedule Modification:** Under the direction of the Stake Presidency and Area Seventy, immediately suspend in-person Sunday services and activities at 368 W Aspen Hills Blvd and 270 W Harvest Hills Blvd. Transition to home-centered sacrament authorization and virtual ward broadcasts.
3. **Care Facility Shielding (Saratoga View):** High Council Care Center Liaison contacts administration at Saratoga View Senior Living (1429 N Utah Cottage Dr) to verify lockdown protocols and confirm zero in-person volunteer visits until cleared by facility medical staff.
4. **Activate Contactless Ministering Check-Ins:** Wards use existing Elders Quorum and Relief Society ministering companionships (via phone, text, and video — no call trees) to identify quarantined families requiring urgent food or medical delivery.
5. **Symptom Monitoring & Triage Guidance:** Disseminate public health hotline numbers and direct members with severe respiratory distress to call 911 or contact Intermountain Saratoga Springs Emergency Services (392 W Medical Dr).

* **Critical Resources Required:** Digital thermometers, pulse oximeters, N95 particulate masks (from library cache), disinfectant wipes, disposable nitrile gloves, virtual streaming links.
* **Lead Stake Callings Assigned:** Stake Presidency, High Council Care Center Liaison, Stake Relief Society Presidency, Assigned Wards.

---

### Mid-Term Needs (1–2 Weeks): Sustained Home Care & Welfare Support
1. **Contactless Doorstep Care Network:** Stake Relief Society Presidency coordinates volunteer drop-offs of prepared nourishing meals, groceries, and essential prescriptions placed on front porches with text notification (zero face-to-face contact to prevent volunteer exposure).
2. **Pulse Oximeter Loaner Program:** Coordinate voluntary sharing of privately owned pulse oximeters to monitor blood oxygen levels for quarantined individuals experiencing respiratory illness (triggering ER evaluation if SpO2 drops below 92%).
3. **Saratoga Springs Bishops' Storehouse Welfare Orders:** Stake Presidency and local Bishops coordinate emergency commodity orders from the Saratoga Springs Bishops' Storehouse (801-436-8205) for quarantined households and families facing sudden wage loss.
4. **Daily Welfare Checks on Isolated Members:** Ensure daily remote check-ins (phone/video) on elderly members living alone, single parents with multiple sick children, and high-anxiety households.
5. **Facility Sanitization Protocols:** High Council Facility Liaisons supervise Church facilities sanitization and air filter replacements at both meetinghouses before any restricted leadership access.

* **Critical Resources Required:** Thermal insulated delivery bags, member-loaned pulse oximeters, Bishops' Storehouse welfare food commodities, EPA-registered viral disinfectants.
* **Lead Stake Callings Assigned:** Stake Relief Society Presidency, High Council Logistics Liaison, High Council Facility Liaisons, Assigned Wards.

---

### Long-Term Needs (2+ Months): Recovery, Rebuilding & Resilience
1. **Bereavement & Grief Support:** Stake Presidency coordinates pastoral care and referrals to LDS Family Services counseling for families experiencing the loss of loved ones or long-term post-viral fatigue.
2. **Phased Return to Assembly:** Implement progressive, phased resumption of in-person church meetings adhering to Church headquarters and Utah Department of Health guidelines.
3. **Replenish Household Preparedness:** Encourage families to replenish depleted home medicine cabinets, electrolytes, masks, and shelf-stable foods as retail supply chains normalize.
4. **Stake After-Action Review:** Document communication bottlenecks, volunteer safety compliance, and community care lessons learned to update the Stake Emergency Operations Plan.

* **Critical Resources Required:** LDS Family Services counselors, fast offering welfare assistance funds, hygiene and cleaning replenishment guides.
* **Lead Stake Callings Assigned:** Stake Presidency, Stake Relief Society Presidency, High Council.

---

## 3. Foundational Principle: Families Start with What They Have

During an epidemic or widespread disease outbreak, the home is the first line of defense. The Stake and Wards do not operate medical clinics or store pharmaceuticals. Every household is encouraged to maintain a basic family "sick-room care kit" with items they already have:

* Over-the-counter fever reducers (acetaminophen, ibuprofen) and pediatric formulations.
* Oral electrolyte rehydration packets, ginger ale, broths, and easy-to-digest shelf-stable foods.
* A working digital thermometer, disposable masks, and hand sanitizer.
* A minimum 2-to-3 week supply of routine prescription medications.
