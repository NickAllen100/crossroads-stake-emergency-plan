# Worksheet 2 (Page 6 Overview): Planning for Disruptions

> **Guideline Compliance:** The planning guide specifies creating a Planning for Disruptions worksheet. Per planning directives, a dedicated Page 6 worksheet has been generated for **each disaster identified on Page 5**, mapping the disruptions into three critical response horizons:
> 1. **Short-Term Needs (0–72 Hours):** Immediate life preservation, rapid triage, member accountability, emergency shut-offs, and initial shelter.
> 2. **Mid-Term Needs (1–2 Weeks):** Sustained food and potable water distribution, waste/sanitation management, host-family housing, and Church welfare integration.
> 3. **Long-Term Needs (2+ Months):** Structural repair and rebuilding, spiritual/emotional trauma care, employment/financial recovery, and long-term community rehabilitation.
