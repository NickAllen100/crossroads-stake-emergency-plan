## Step 1: Medical & Auxiliary Power Self-Check (Do This First)

If anyone in your home relies on electricity, refrigeration, mobility equipment, or daily medication, check your readiness before a disaster strikes:

| Household Need | What Your Family Should Have Ready at Home | Coordinate with Utility & Ward |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SN-1: Medical Power** *(Oxygen concentrator, CPAP, ventilator, feeding pump)* | **Do you have auxiliary power?** Maintain a portable lithium battery station (500–1000Wh+ with solar panel) or outdoor inverter generator sized for your device's wattage for 24–72 hours. Test twice a year. | Enroll in **Rocky Mountain Power (1-877-508-5088)** medical priority list; notify your Bishopric/RS confidentially (Form W-1). |
| **SN-2: Cold-Chain Meds** *(Insulin, biologics)* | Insulated medical pouch or small hard cooler + frozen gel packs ready for 4+ hour summer blackouts; 2–3 week prescription buffer. | Notify your ministering companion / Ward RS confidentially. |
| **SN-3 to SN-5: Mobility, Dialysis & Seniors** | Backup manual transfer plan, written clinic backup schedules, medical ID tags (if memory-care risk), packed go-bag by exit. | Pre-arrange a nearby neighbor or ministering companion with an accessible vehicle. |
| **SN-6 to SN-8: Infants, Sensory & Language** | 2 weeks of infant formula/diapers + dedicated safe water; noise-canceling headphones/comfort items; translated alert apps. | Keep your contact info updated in the **Church Member Tools** app. |

## Step 2: Water & Food Storage (*Utah Area Guide*, pp. 5, 11–14)

### Water Storage (Top Priority)
* **Baseline (72 Hours):** At least **1 gallon (4 liters) per person per day** = 3 gallons per person.
* **Target Goal (2 Weeks):** Where possible, store **14 gallons per person** (more in hot weather or for infant formula).
* **Safe Storage Rules:** Use sturdy, leak-proof, break-resistant food-grade containers. **Do NOT reuse plastic milk or juice jugs.** Keep containers out of sunlight, away from heat, and **off cement/ground** (store on a board/shelf where a leak won't damage your home).
* **Purification & Local Saratoga Springs Rules:** Store a water filter or unscented bleach. Know how to isolate your **40–50 gallon home water heater** (turn off incoming cold valve). **NEVER drink pressurized secondary irrigation water** (untreated Utah Lake water). Bring questionable tap water to a rolling boil for **3 full minutes** at Saratoga Springs' 4,500 ft elevation.

### Food Storage (Build Gradually, Without Debt)
* **72-Hour Grab-and-Go Food:** Ready-to-eat, non-refrigerated foods (+ infant formula if needed).
* **2-Week to 3-Month Pantry:** Start with a **1-week supply** of everyday shelf-stable foods your family actually eats; build gradually to 2 weeks and 3 months by buying 1–2 extra items each grocery trip. Rotate oldest first.
* **Longer-Term Staples (30-Year Shelf Life):** Grains (wheat, white rice, oats, pasta), legumes (beans, lentils), sugar/honey, dry milk, cooking oil, salt/seasonings, multivitamins, baking basics + manual can opener/grinder. Local resources: **Saratoga Springs Home Storage Center (142 W Medical Dr, Suite 1, Saratoga Springs • 801-436-8205)** & `foodstorage.ChurchofJesusChrist.org`.
* **Alternative Cooking (*Utah Area Guide*, p. 5):** Canned heat/candle warmers, fireplace/wood stove, or **outdoor-only** propane grills/camp stoves (strictly outdoors — lethal carbon monoxide hazard indoors).

## Step 3: 72-Hour Emergency Kit — Home, Vehicle & Work (*Utah Area Guide*, p. 6)

Pack portable kits in backpacks or wheeled bins near an exit, plus smaller kits for vehicles and work/school. Review twice a year:
1. **Shelter & Warmth:** Tents/tarps, blankets/sleeping bags, seasonal clothing layers, sturdy shoes beside every bed.
2. **Food & Water:** 3 days simple ready-to-eat food, 3 gallons water/person (or portable filter), manual can opener, infant/pet supplies.
3. **Light & Power:** Flashlights/headlamps, extra batteries, charged phone power bank, matches/candles.
4. **Communication:** Battery/hand-crank NOAA Weather Radio, FRS walkie-talkies (Ward Channels **1, 3, 5**), printed contact card.
5. **Medical & Hygiene:** First-aid kit, 2–3 weeks of prescriptions, OTC fever reducers, N95 masks, soap, toilet paper, feminine supplies, twin-bucket toilet bags.
6. **Emergency Cash:** Small bills and coins (for when ATMs and card readers are offline).
7. **Important Documents:** Waterproof folder + encrypted USB/cloud backup (IDs, property/vehicle records, insurance policies, medical/Rx info, contact list).
8. **Entertainment & Comfort:** Scriptures, books, games, coloring books/crayons for children.

## Step 4: Preparing Your Home & Utility Shut-Offs (*Utah Area Guide*, p. 5)

* **Natural Gas:** Tether a non-sparking wrench at your gas meter. **ONLY shut off gas if you smell gas, hear hissing, or see the meter dial spinning rapidly.** Only **Enbridge Gas Utah (1-800-767-1689)** may safely turn it back on.
* **Culinary Water & Water Heater:** Know your main indoor shut-off valve (burst pipes) and your water heater cold-inlet valve (saves 40–50 gal of potable water).
* **Electricity:** Label circuits and show adults/teens how to flip the main breaker safely.
* **Local Saratoga Springs Hazard Quick-Prep:**
  * *Earthquake:* Strap water heater & tall furniture to studs; keep twin-bucket toilet kit (two 5-gal buckets, heavy bags, pine shavings/litter).
  * *Wildfire (Lake Mountain):* Sign up for City Everbridge alerts; clear dry grass/combustibles away from decks & fences; ready to evacuate north in 15 minutes.
  * *Winter Blizzard/Freeze:* Insulate vulnerable pipes, disconnect hoses; keep battery CO detector & warm-room bedding (zero indoor grills/generators).
  * *Windstorm:* Anchor trampolines with 15-inch helical earth augers; keep a heavy tarp on hand.
  * *Hazmat Plume (Redwood Rd):* Keep 4–6 mil plastic sheeting + duct tape to seal an interior room; shut off HVAC/exhaust fans immediately; do NOT drive to school during a plume.
  * *Widespread Disease:* Keep home sick-room kit (fever reducers, electrolytes, thermometer, masks, 2–3 weeks of prescriptions).

## Step 5: Family Communication & Gathering Plan (*Utah Area Guide*, p. 9)

* **Update Member Tools:** Keep your household phone numbers and email current in the Church **Member Tools** app so Ward and Ministering leaders can reach you.
* **Out-of-Town Contact:** Choose a relative/friend outside Utah County whom every family member texts/calls to relay location and condition when local lines jam.
* **Ministering Accountability (No Call Trees):** Once your family is safe, check on your assigned ministering families and immediate next-door neighbors (on foot if phones are down) and report status to EQ/RS leadership.
* **Designated Crossroads Stake Gathering & Reunification Sites:**
